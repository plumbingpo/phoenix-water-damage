Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across Phoenix, Phoenix Restoration Co handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number

When to Call for Water Extraction in Phoenix

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
