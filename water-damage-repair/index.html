Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Photograph damaged materials before they are removed in case your insurer asks later.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Phoenix

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Following water from above, part of the ceiling droops, has cracks or crumbles when touched.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.

How Our Water Damage Repair Works

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Verify Dryness First

Final moisture readings are taken on the subfloor, framing and any drywall left in place. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

2

Write the Scope

You receive a room by room list of what will be replaced, the materials we plan to use and the expected schedule. When there is an open claim, the scope is coordinated with your adjuster.

3

Rebuild the Structure

We refill open cavities with insulation, hang, tape and finish drywall, and repair the subfloor. After that we install trim, cabinetry and floors, ordering the work so completed surfaces are not damaged.

4

Finish and Walk Through

Texture is matched, walls are primed and painted, and baseboards, doors and hardware go back on. Together we then walk through each room, and every listed item is corrected before the job is closed.

The Benefits of Professional Water Damage Repair

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry.
