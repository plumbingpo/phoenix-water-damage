Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Phoenix Restoration Co handles emergency flood cleanup across Phoenix and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
